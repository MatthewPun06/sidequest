# User Experience

## Personas

### College Students

 * Motivations:
     * Want community and friendship and belonging
     * Want career success in the future
  
 * Frustrations:
     * Not feeling connected with their friends
     * Feeling back inconveniencing their friends
     * Needing to plan out hang outs and taking valuable time doing it

 * Goals:
     * Spend less time planning and more time actually connecting
     * Get to build friendships and community with less coordination
     * Knowing when other people are close by and hang out spontaneously

 * Empathy Map:
    
    1) Says
         * “I want to meet more people, but everyone seems busy all the time.”
         * “It shouldn’t take this much effort just to make plans with friends.”
         * “I’m trying to balance school, social life, and preparing for my future.”
         * “I miss when hanging out happened naturally instead of being scheduled weeks ahead.”
        
    2) Does:
         * Checks group chats frequently to coordinate plans and stay connected.
         * Joins clubs, study groups, or campus events to meet people and build community.
         * Balances coursework, internships, and social activities throughout the year.
         * Makes spontaneous plans when friends are nearby or available between classes.
        
    3) Thinks:
         * “I want strong friendships, not just casual classmates or online connections.”
         * “Planning every hangout makes socializing feel exhausting sometimes.”
         * “Being around the right people could improve both my college experience and future opportunities.”
         * “I wish there were easier ways to know when friends are free or nearby.”
        
    4) Feels: 
         * Excited when forming new friendships or discovering a sense of belonging.
         * Overwhelmed by balancing academics, career pressure, and social life.
         * Frustrated by the time and coordination required to organize hangouts.
         * Lonely or disconnected when social opportunities hangouts.
         * Lonely or disconnected when social opportunities fall through or friendships feel distant.

### Young Workers (22-30)
    
 * Motivations:
     * Want life outside of work and to experience life (balance)
     * Form communities, try new things, and live while they are young
     * Wanting to keep old relationships strong

 * Frustrations:
     * Having a monotonous life of work, eat, sleep
     * Not feeling connected or having close friendships
     * Not having the energy to do more
     * Not having time to hang out with friends
    
 * Goal:
     * Break up the monotony of work life with new things
     * Form connections in adulthood
     * Make new experiences while you are young
     * Find times to connect with friends

 * Empathy Map
    1) Says:
         * “I don’t want my whole life to just be work and errands.”
         * “It’s so hard to keep up with friends now that everyone’s busy.”
         * “I want to try new things while I’m still young enough to enjoy them.”
         * “By the end of the workday, I barely have energy for anything else.”

    2) Does:
         * Scrolls social media looking for local events, activities, or travel ideas.
         * Tries to make plans with friends, but often deals with scheduling conflicts.
         * Balances work responsibilities with hobbies, fitness, or side interests.
         * Uses weekends or days off to recharge, socialize, or explore new experiences.

    3) Thinks:
         * “I need a better balance between making money and actually enjoying life.”
         * “Making genuine friendships as an adult feels harder than I expected.”
         * “If I don’t take opportunities now, I might regret wasting my 20s later.”
         * “I wish there were easier ways to stay connected with people consistently.”

    4) Feels:
         * Drained from repetitive work routines and daily responsibilities.
         * Excited by opportunities for new experiences, travel, and social activities.
         * Lonely or disconnected when friendships fade due to busy schedules.
         * Hopeful building a meaningful social life and creating lasting memories.

## User Journey

### Introduction
 1) Open app
 2) What the app is about (shared calendar + activities + spontaneous notifications)
 3) Sign in/Make Account
 4) Name, Email, Phone, Notifications, Location services
 5) Add calendar integrations
 6) Add friends from contacts
 7) Start Loop!

### Features
 1) calendar
     * friend/group organized calendars (color coded)
     * highlighted free time (pulsing)
     * select block of free time!
     * you can also select blocks of time as your "me" time (weekly, daily, etc); recommended for cooking time, laundry, decompression/destress, and alone time
 2) friend groups 
     * create group (+ name)
     * add friends to the group
 3) event planning
     * name
     * datetime
     * place
     * participants (equal to the the group the free time was clicked with)
     * add other friends (list all friends with a search, filter, and sort feature) (most recent)
 4) nearby notification
     * click on notification
     * gives you a "find-my" kind of compass to track your friend (direction + distance)
     * gives you a chat area
     * NO MAP EVER, part of the fun and to not give direct locations (especially from afar)
     * once you leave for 30 minutes, then it restarts and notifications can happen again
     * no notifications when you are doing something (when time is blocked in your calendar)
 5) event planning/new things to do
     * recommendations based on your interests (art, physical activity, music, food, hosting, etc)
     * also adds recommendations when creating events
     * allows you to plan something from here as well and invite whoever is free (or not)


### Flow

 * planning
     1) onboard
     2) select free time from calendar
     3) select/name activity
     4) add friends
     5) send invite
     6) hang out!

 * spontaneous
     1) see a friend who is free nearby
     2) find them/text them
     3) hang out!

### Feelings

1) low stakes
     * should feel low stakes
     * no additional pressure to hang out
     * encouragement to add "me time"
     * only busy times are shown (no reason why)
     * nearby notifications are optional and friend specific (you need to choose)
     * rejection doesn't feel bad (no shaming for rejecting or too much encouragement to accept)
     * invites persist in case you change your mind
2) fulfilment
     * send gratitude to inviter
     * journaling? reflection?
     * no streak, but maybe a friendship level
     * reminders to hang out with close friends
3) excitement
     * they should feel comfortable and excited to hang out with their friends
     * it shouldn't be stressful to open the app, it should be super smooth
     * they want something more in life and to get energy from what they do

## Style Guide

1) Colors (pastel)
     * purple: comfort, magical, creative (it creates the safe space and lets you reflect on something whimsical that you want to do)
     * yellow: excitement, optimism, exploration, and spontaneous (the side quest part of the color scheme where friends may be nearby or do something new)

2) Shape
     * rounded shapes: communicate a sense of whimsy, youthfulness, comfort

3) Fonts
     * Quicksand: accents/titles
     * Nunito: regular text
     * Caveat: fun text
     * rounded and sans-serif so it is easily readable, but also playful and comforting
